Features

Everything the bid process needs.

Built specifically for small to mid-market contractors. No bloat. No enterprise pricing.

📄

Blueprint Parser

GPT-4o Vision reads any PDF blueprint and extracts a structured list of scope items — drywall, electrical, plumbing, and more — in seconds.

⚡ Saves 2–3 hrs/project
✉️

Bid Email Generator

AI drafts professional bid request emails for each subcontractor scope. You review, customize, and send — all from one dashboard.

⚡ Saves 1–2 hrs/project
📊

Live Bid Tracker

Real-time dashboard shows every bid's status — received, pending, or overdue. Automated follow-up emails sent without lifting a finger.

⚡ Saves 2–3 hrs/week
🔔

Deadline Alerts

Never miss a bid deadline again. Automated alerts at 48 hours, 24 hours, and the moment a response goes late — via email and dashboard.

⚡ Eliminates missed bids
📅

Schedule Generator

Once bids are confirmed, AI maps scope items into a project schedule with phases, dependencies, and timelines. Export to Sheets, CSV, or PDF.

⚡ Saves 3–5 hrs/project
🤖

Email Triage Assistant

n8n workflow monitors your inbox, auto-routes bid responses to the tracker, drafts replies to subcontractor questions, and flags urgent client messages.
